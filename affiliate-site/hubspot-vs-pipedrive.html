Before you compare: three questions

A CRM fixes a specific leak. If you cannot name the leak, a new tool will not fix it. Write down the answer to each of these first.

  1. Where does customer information get lost? An inbox, a spreadsheet, one person's head.
  2. Which follow-ups are late, again and again? Quotes, renewals, replies to new leads.
  3. Which of those could a reminder or an automatic email handle? This tells you which plan tier you need.

The third answer is the one that sets your price, because both products hold their stronger automation back for higher tiers.

Where HubSpot gets expensive

HubSpot is cheap to start and costly to grow. The free CRM covers up to two users, and the Starter plan is priced for small teams. The jump comes at Professional, where the per-seat price rises several times over and the onboarding fee applies.

This is the most common complaint in public reviews. Small-business reviewers on Capterra say it “can get expensive quickly once you want the automation features”, and that custom reporting and deeper automation sit in the higher tiers.

If the free or Starter plan covers what you wrote down above, HubSpot is hard to beat on price. If you already know you need advanced workflows, price the Professional tier before you commit your data to it.

Where Pipedrive gets expensive

Pipedrive has no free plan, so you pay from the first month after the trial. The per-seat price rises in even steps between tiers, with no setup fee, and the cost creeps up through add-ons and through moving up a tier for automation.

Reviewers often describe Pipedrive as intuitive, including people who moved to it from HubSpot. The complaints are different: some report a fiddly initial setup, duplicate records, and pipelines that get crowded with activities.

Pipedrive is built around the sales pipeline. If you also want email marketing, landing pages or a help desk in the same tool, you will be adding other products next to it.

How to decide in an afternoon

  1. Take your three answers from above.
  2. Open HubSpot's free CRM and a Pipedrive trial. Both can be started without paying.
  3. Add five real customers and one real deal to each.
  4. Set up the one follow-up that is late most often. Note which plan each product needs for it.
  5. Use the calculator above with that plan and your team size.

Choose the one your team opened without being asked. A CRM nobody updates costs more than either price on this page.
